Replication of Fomby, Ikeda & Loayza (2013), “The Growth Aftermath of Natural Disasters”

Econometrics II (graduate), Spring 2026

Python replication of a Journal of Applied Econometrics paper that uses a fixed-effects panel VARX model to estimate how GDP growth responds to droughts, floods, earthquakes and storms. I replicated the implementation of Bootstrap Bias Correction for the Nickell bias in dynamic panels to obtain bias-corrected impulse responses. I translated the original MATLAB code to Python and sped up the nested bootstrap with Numba.

Code · Report · Slides

Wage Inequality in Turkey

Following the Review of Economic Dynamics guidelines, we used Turkish micro-level data (SILC and Household Budget Survey) to estimate inequality indices and to analyze wage premiums and informality.

Latent Space Manipulation and Conditional Latent Diffusion with StyleGAN2

Probabilistic Approach to Machine Learning, Fall 2025 — with Hüseyin Çiyik

Semantic facial attribute editing in the latent space of a pre-trained StyleGAN2-ADA generator. We compare linear edits in the Z and W spaces (Mean Difference vs. Linear SVM directions) with a conditional latent diffusion model that uses SDE-Edit and classifier-free guidance.
